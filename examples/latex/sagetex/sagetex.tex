\begin{sagesilent}
f(x) = x^3 - 2*x + 1
g = f.diff(x)
\end{sagesilent}

The derivative of $f(x) = x^3 - 2x + 1$ is $f'(x) = \sage{g(x)}$, and
$\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx = \sage{integral(f(x), x, 0, 1)}$.

The 10th prime is $\sage{nth_prime(10)}$, and $2^{100} = \sage{2^100}$.
